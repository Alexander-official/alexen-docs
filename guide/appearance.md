# Appearance & settings

## Appearance

**Appearance** changes the panel's theme (light, dark or system), accent colour and layout details. It is saved per browser.

![Appearance](/img/theme.webp)

You can also change the panel language (English, Türkçe, Русский, فارسی, Türkmençe, 中文) from the button at the bottom of the menu.

## Brand

**Settings → Brand** renames the panel for your customers: the name shown in the sidebar, footer and subscription page, your company name and the support contact.

![Brand](/img/brand.webp)

## Documentation page

**Documentation** inside the panel is a short guide for your role (admin or sudo), the subscription link format and the API.

## My profile

Your name, picture, short bio and contact, and your own password. Resellers also see their quotas here.

![Profile](/img/profile.webp)

## External panels

**Settings → External panel** connects other panels (3x-ui, s-ui and similar) so their users and traffic appear in Alexen.

![External panels](/img/external-panels.webp)
