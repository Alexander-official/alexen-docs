# Overview & statistics

## Overview

The whole panel at a glance, from hourly history. Pick 24 hours, 7, 30, 90 days, one year or a custom range.

![Overview](/img/overview.webp)

- **Users, online now, traffic, expiring soon, new users, subscription updates.**
- **Traffic over time**, per server or stacked, and the protocol split.
- **Online users and IPs over time.**
- **Top users** and the servers' load.
- The license card shows days left and your limits.

## Statistics

**Statistics** shows traffic per inbound, per transport (tcp, xhttp, ...) and the heaviest users.

![Statistics](/img/statistics.webp)

## Nodes usage

**Nodes → Nodes Usage** shows uplink and downlink per node for a chosen period.

![Nodes usage](/img/nodes-usage.webp)
